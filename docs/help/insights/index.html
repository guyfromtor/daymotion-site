Weather And Elevation

Start with the summary, then tap into the details.

The weather and elevation graphs first show summarized stats so you can read the overall pattern quickly. Tap a stop number or time record when you want to inspect one moment in the journey, then tap the selected stop again to return to the summary.

1Read the weather summary

The weather graph starts with the main temperature range and conditions recorded during the journey. Use it to see whether the day warmed, cooled, cleared, or became windier as stops changed.

2Tap a weather stop number

Numbered stop badges connect the graph to the journey timeline. Tap a stop number to see the weather recorded for that stop, including temperature, condition, wind, and the stop name. Tap the selected stop again to return to the summary.

3Read the elevation summary

The elevation graph begins with route-level stats such as high point, low point, climb, and descent. These summarize the vertical effort before you inspect individual measurements.

4Tap an elevation record

Elevation uses both stop numbers and time records. Tap a stop number for stop-specific altitude, or tap a time record to see the selected measurement and why it matters, such as highest point, lowest point, climb, or descent. Tap the selected stop or record again to return to the summary.

Questions

Common Insights questions

Why is longest stop highlighted?

Duration often explains the most meaningful pause or the main activity in a journey.

Where do mood values come from?

Mood insights use the moods saved on stops in the journey.

What happens when I tap a stop number?

The graph selects that stop and shows the detail behind the summary, such as the stop weather or stop elevation. Tap the selected stop again to go back to the summary.

What is a time record?

A time record is an elevation measurement from the route. It lets the graph show important points even when they happen between stops.

Can I edit insights directly?

Edit the source details in Stops or Journal. Insights update from that journey data.

Insights summarize route, stop, mood, weather, and elevation data. Treat them as part of the journey when sharing.
