Two Ways To Share

Choose how the memory reaches them.

Send a journey page

Share the story of your day, with a route map, photos, and memories from the stops you choose. Send it to friends or family, or save a copy to enjoy later.

They can explore your journey in their web browser without needing DayMotion. Your photos travel with it, so everything stays together.

Email a summary

A short journey overview in the email, with the complete generated journey page attached. It gives someone a quick introduction and a fuller memory to open when they have time.

DayMotion opens a draft in Mail for you to address and send. A configured Mail account is required. Your stop selection determines the detailed stops in the attachment.

Choose What To Share

Tell the whole story, or choose the moments.

After choosing a format, select the stops to include. Start with a preset, then tap individual stops to adjust your selection.

All

Include every stop in the journey.

Journal or Photos

Choose stops with written journal text, photos, or both.

Journal

Choose stops with written journal text. A mood on its own does not qualify.

Photos

Choose stops with photos. Their other available details are included too.

Make your own selection

Tap stops to include or exclude them. Use None to clear the selection and start again. Choose at least one stop before generating your share.

For example

Choose just Love Park and Niagara Falls to share those two stops’ photos and memories. The route map and journey overview still provide the wider context of the day.

You are selecting stops, not switching individual kinds of content on and off. Selecting Photos does not create a photos-only export. Deselecting a stop removes its detailed entry; the broader route, overview, and insights can still reflect the whole journey.

Inside Your Journey Page

More than a collection of photos.

DayMotion Export & Share

Your journeys are free. Sharing them beautifully is optional.

Record your journeys, add and edit your memories, and revisit them in DayMotion for free. Pay for Export & Share when you want to create a finished journey page or email summary to keep or share outside the app.

Available plans and prices appear in the app’s purchase screen.

Create your first share

  1. Open Library and tap Share on a journey.
  2. Choose Send a journey page or Email a summary.
  3. Select the stops you want to include.
  4. Tap Send Journey Page or Email Summary. If needed, the app will offer Export & Share before creating the output.
  5. Choose where to save or send the page, or finish your email in Mail.

Before You Send

A few useful details.

Does the recipient need DayMotion?

No. They can explore your journey in their web browser. If their messaging or email app only shows a preview, they can save the attached journey and open it in a browser to see the full page.

Can I share only some stops?

Yes. Choose a preset or select individual stops. Their detailed entries appear in the journey page; the route and journey-level information can still describe the wider journey.

Why can’t I email a summary?

Set up an account in Mail first, or choose Send a journey page and use another compatible destination.

What am I paying for?

Export & Share lets you create journey pages and email summaries. Recording, editing, and revisiting your journeys in DayMotion remain free.

Review the journey before sharing: a file may include your route, stop names, photos, journal text, moods, weather, and timing. Only send it to people or services you are comfortable sharing those details with.
