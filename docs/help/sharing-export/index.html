Sharing Methods

Each method is built for a different destination.

Send a journey page

Create a shareable journey page for Messages, WhatsApp, AirDrop, or another app in the iOS share sheet.

Email a summary

Open an email with a short summary and the full journey page attached.

Create Instagram Story Pack

Create vertical story-ready images and save them so you can post them through Instagram.

Choose Stops

Pick the parts of the journey that should travel with the share.

All

Includes every stop in the journey.

Journal or Photos

Includes stops that have notes, moods, tags, or photos attached.

Journal

Includes stops with written or mood detail, even if they do not have photos.

Photos

Includes stops that have photos attached.

Manual selection

Tap individual stops when you want a custom set.

None

Clears the current selection so you can start again.

What Selection Changes

The same stop choices affect each method differently.

Choose fewer stops when the share should feel focused. Choose more stops when the recipient needs the full route and context.

Journey Page

The selected stops appear in the shared journey page with their route context, timing, photos, journal text, weather, and mood details when available.

Email Summary

The selected stops shape the email summary and the attached full journey page.

Instagram Story Pack

The selected stops become story frames. If the selection would create more than 100 frames, choose fewer stops before generating the pack.

Tips

Review the share before sending it on.

Start focused

Use Journal or Photos when you only want the meaningful stops to appear.

Check names

Review stop names and notes before sharing them with someone else.

Use Story Pack for social

Story Pack images are designed for vertical story posting, not long-form review.

Questions

Common questions

Which method should I choose?

Use a journey page for general sharing, an email summary when the recipient expects an email, and Instagram Story Pack when you want story-ready images.

Can I share only part of a journey?

Yes. Choose a preset or tap individual stops before generating the share.

Why does Story Pack warn me about too many frames?

Instagram Stories work best with a limited number of images. DayMotion asks you to choose fewer stops when the pack would exceed 100 frames.

Before you share, review the selected stops. A share may include route shape, stop names, photos, journal text, weather, mood, and timing.
