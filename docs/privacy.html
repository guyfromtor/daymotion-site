DayMotion Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 8, 2026

Public policy URL: https://guyfromtor.github.io/daymotion-site/privacy.html

DayMotion helps you record and review personal journeys using location, stops, notes, moods, photos, weather context, and related trip details. This policy explains what DayMotion handles and how you can control your data.

Information DayMotion Stores

DayMotion may store journey information including:

  • Journey names, dates, routes, stops, distances, durations, and movement context.
  • Location samples used to track your route and detect stops.
  • Notes, mood entries, and other journey details you add.
  • Photos you attach to stops or save to Apple Photos.
  • Weather information associated with journeys or stops.
  • Settings and preferences needed to run the app.

Location And Motion

DayMotion uses location to track your route and detect stops. DayMotion uses motion and step-related signals to improve journey tracking when GPS is unavailable or less reliable.

Photos

DayMotion uses the camera and photo access only for journey memories you choose to capture, attach, or save. If you enable saving camera photos to Apple Photos, DayMotion can add those photos to your Apple Photos library.

Weather

DayMotion may use Apple Weather data to show weather context for journeys and stops. Weather data is used to support journey summaries and is not used for advertising or tracking.

iCloud Sync

Journey data is stored on your device and may sync with your private iCloud account when iCloud is available. DayMotion does not provide a separate external account system.

Tracking, Analytics, And Advertising

DayMotion does not use third-party analytics SDKs, advertising SDKs, or tracking SDKs. DayMotion does not track you across apps or websites owned by other companies.

Sharing And Exports

DayMotion can export or share journey content when you choose to do so. Shared exports may include journey details, locations, photos, notes, moods, weather context, and other content you selected.

Deleting Data

You can delete journey data from inside DayMotion. If iCloud sync is enabled, deletion may sync through your private iCloud account. You can also contact DayMotion for privacy questions or deletion requests.

Children

DayMotion is not designed to collect information from children.

Changes

This policy may be updated when DayMotion's features or data practices change. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when the policy changes.

Contact

For privacy questions or deletion requests, contact daymotion.app@gmail.com.
